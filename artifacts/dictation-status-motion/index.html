Shared behavior contract

ListeningUpdate from microphone peak or RMS samples at 10 to 15 Hz. Silence settles near the center line.
ProcessingRun one transition on state entry, 300 to 650 ms, then stop animating.
CompletionUse one short settle or check draw. Keep the terminal state visible long enough to read.
Reduced motionKeep color, label, and static level shape. Remove every transition without losing meaning.
Constraint: orange means recording, fallback, or action needed.Implementation note: prefer CSS transforms and SVG point updates. No canvas loop unless the audio event rate requires it.
